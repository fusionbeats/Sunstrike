Modes

  • Duel. First to three rounds. One life per round.
  • Deathmatch. First to ten eliminations, with quick respawns.
  • Control. Capture the point, then hold it until your meter reaches 100%.
  • King of the Hill. Earn points while you stand on the hill alone. It moves every 40 seconds.
  • Mystery Duel. Like a duel, but both of you get a random hero every round.

Tips

  • Dealing damage charges your ultimate. Watch the ring next to your health.
  • Health packs sit around every map. Big ones heal a lot more.
  • You get a short shield after spawning. Shooting ends it early.
  • Standing in the enemy’s spawn area burns you, so don’t camp it.
